NORFOLK SOUTHERN TRAIN DERAILS

Train 32 of Norfolk Southern 7 was en route from Madison, Illinois to Conway, Pennsylvania and comprised of 149 rail cars. Continuing its journey on the Fort Wain rail line, the train derailed in East Palestine, Ohio, on February 3rd, around 9 p.m. due to an increase of pressure on the wheel.
